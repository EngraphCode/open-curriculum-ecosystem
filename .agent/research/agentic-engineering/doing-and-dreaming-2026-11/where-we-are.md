## 1. What the four say together

One diagnosis appears in all four, each time in different words.

| Piece | Its words |
| --- | --- |
| A | Every refinement step starts at the owner's word or an agent's recall |
| B | "learning from accumulated experience happens only when a person remembers to start it" |
| C | The sources "place the stop and the verdict outside the thing that does the work"; none describes a standing intention to learn |
| D | "every step-back instrument depends on the working agent deciding to use it" |

One cure shape appears in all four as well. D states it best: "something outside the agent's
recall marked the moment, and the response was a thing that had to be produced or could not be
passed". A's starter that also does the work, B's clock with a standing brief, and C's
admission of a lesson only after running it are the same shape at different moments.

The Practice wrote this down itself in June. PDR-098 names the empty place (a check fired by
the environment, recognised by judgement, answered by advice) and forbids filling it with
another always-on rule. Five months and four research efforts later the place is still empty.

## 2. What reflection changed

Four things I held before reading all of it together, and no longer hold as I did.

1. **The agreement is weaker than it looks.** Four seats reaching one conclusion felt like four
   confirmations. Most of the estate-side evidence in A, D and my reading of B is the same
   three records: the two archive syntheses and the retrospective of 2026-09-16. "Sixteen of
   nineteen", the growth of about 30 % and "the napkin has become a journal" recur across A, D
   and the readings of B. C and D both found, separately, that agreement among readers of one frame is
   one piece of evidence. That applies to this programme. What is independent: C's papers,
   Hermes's code, the owner's own reasoning in B, and the few things anyone ran.
2. **Almost nothing was run.** Across the four pieces the first-hand trials number three: D's
   one scenario with and without one rule, the October skill-evaluation run the comparison
   cites, and one hook firing in this session. The rest is reading. The `reason` skill's own
   gate says that when cumulative inquiry reaches the cost of the direct trial it has lost its
   warrant. D's trial cost a quarter of a dollar an arm and under twenty seconds. This
   programme has spent that many times over on reading about whether written lessons work.
3. **The programme is an instance of what it studies.** The estate's measured response to
   failure is to write. Its response to finding that out has been four reports, a strategic
   node and three readings. I wrote several of them. The owner's "plan to make a plan" was a
   deliberate hold and not this; my adding a reading for each result as it arrived was.
4. **A premise under PDR-098, the comparison and both syntheses may be false on this
   platform.** All of them treat a rule with a home as text in the agent's context that fails
   at the moment of the act. D measured what the Claude adapters deliver: one line per rule,
   "Read and follow" and a path. At session start the model holds 103 file names. In D's one
   trial the rule's file was never opened. If that is typical, "written lessons recur" is
   partly "written lessons are not delivered", which is a different fault with a cheaper cure,
   and it changes what forgetting means: a rule nobody opens is already forgotten in practice
   and still costs its line. One trial, one tier, a session not opened with start-right. It is
   the highest-value unknown in the whole body of work, because every lane's diagnosis rests
   on it.

**Added the same day, after step 1 of section 5 was run.** The owner asked for the delivery
measurement and it is recorded in [`delivery-trial.md`](delivery-trial.md). Item 4 above is
answered in part: rules are opened. Seven of ten sessions opened at least one rule, 11
of 124 rules were opened at all, and most opened rules had file names that shared words with
the task; how a session chose was not measured. Rules named for a habit of mind were not opened by tasks that did not use their
words. So the premise is neither sound nor false: delivery works for rules about a named
activity and fails for rules about the frame of the work, which are the ones the Attendant
research found the owner supplying by hand. The "Deliver" row of the table below should now
read: one trial of ten sessions, partial and following the task's subject.

## 3. Where we are, as a first sketch

The owner's phrase is self-governance and self-improvement. Read across the four pieces, a
system governs and improves itself when each of these links is closed without a person. The
columns are a first sketch from what was read; the fourth column says how each is known. This

| Start the next round | Nothing in the repository | The owner | Observed 2026-11-12 |

Three things stand out from the table.

- **Authority has been delegated ahead of initiation and verification.** The estate lets an
  agent decide a graduation and lets clean, green work merge without the owner's word. It does
  not let anything start without the owner, and the owner remains the one check on purpose.
  Self-governance here is furthest along on who may decide and least along on who begins and
  who checks.
- **The sources of independence are few and can be listed.** C and D agree that a second
  reader of the same model is weak evidence. What is left that does not depend on the governed
  agent's own judgement: the owner's recorded words; execution, meaning a test or a gate; a
  model of another family; and a held-back scenario. Any mechanism for self-governance is a
  way of bringing one of those four to a moment.
- **Six lanes now stand on this ground.** The Attendant; self-starting refinement;
  outcome-informed Practice learning; the three consolidation nodes of September; the
  action-time interrupt design space; and PDR-130's predictions. They were ratified or written
  between June and November and they share one diagnosis. For the three consolidation nodes a
  search by name found no implementation; the design-space plan's todos are all pending. Nobody
  has set the six side by side.

## 4. What the problem is

Stated as a gap and not as a solution. The estate does not have one account, backed by
measurement, of which of the links above work without the owner. It has slice accounts that
share sources, written by seats each holding one lane. The cost falls on the owner, who is the
starter, the frame check and the reviewer at once; and on every session, which carries a
doctrine corpus whose delivery has not been measured. Success is a map in which each link is
marked measured or unmeasured, and a short order of trials that turns the second kind into the
first.

## 5. Suggested next steps

In order. Each names what warrants it and what would show it wrong. None was begun when this
was written. Step 1 has since been run and is recorded in
[`delivery-trial.md`](delivery-trial.md), with its result in the addition to section 2; it is
not to be run again. The steps after it are as written, and what followed them is in the
[review of the collection](review-of-the-collection.md).

1. **Measure delivery before anything is designed.** Using D's method, run a small set of
   headless sessions, opened as the estate's sessions are opened, and read from their traces
   which rule files, skill bodies and directives were opened. *Warrant:* section 2, item 4; it
   is cheap, contained and reversible, and its result changes every lane's diagnosis.
   *Falsifier:* the traces show rule bodies are opened as a matter of course, in which case
   delivery is sound and the fault is at the act, as the estate has believed. This starts
   sessions, so it needs the owner's word.
2. **Write the deep comparison as the map in section 3, with cells filled by observation.** One
   document, led by the table, covering B, C and D against the Practice's present mechanisms
   link by link, and absorbing the loop baseline's comparison with the Practice, which that
   work left for the owner's go-ahead. *Warrant:* the owner's sentence; six lanes on one
   ground. *Falsifier:* the finished map changes no lane's next step. Then it was a fifth
   essay and the programme should stop writing.
3. **Have it checked by something that is not this model.** This seat wrote A and two of the
   readings; every review in all four pieces was by the same model family. One pass by a model
   of another family, or the owner's own read, is the independent check C and D both say is
   missing. *Warrant:* C's verdict 7; D's reading of the Cricket tallies. *Falsifier:* the
   other reader finds nothing the same-model reviews had not.
4. **At the review of the collection, order the lanes by dependency.** My reading: stepping back inside a
   task (the Attendant's next step, which has an opener and scenarios ready) comes before
   self-started refinement, because D shows that guidance produced between tasks still has to
   be delivered and to fire, and that is the unsolved part. A self-started pass whose first
   work is to decide and drain is the exception, since it subtracts. *Warrant:* D: "A dreaming
   pass that ends in better guidance inherits question 3's whole problem". *Falsifier:* step
   1 shows delivery is sound, which weakens the dependency.
5. **Agree one instrument for "did it take".** D's scenarios, run with and without the thing
   under test, serve the Attendant's mechanism step, the report's eighth part and the
   self-starting experiment alike, and C names that comparison as the field's one worked
   method. *Warrant:* three lanes need the same instrument and one exists. *Falsifier:* the
   scenarios cannot be staged where the recorded cure is absent, which D's trial met once.
6. **Clear what is already owed.** The finished Attendant research plan is still in the live
   plans folder. The consolidation has been due since 19 October. This pull request is green
   and still a draft. None of these needs research.

What I would not do next: commission another reading, or begin either design before step 1
returns.
